P1 — Before Launch · Phase 5
Build Retailer / Authorized Dealer Pages at /retailers/[slug]
Bez Ambar has ~35 authorized retailers on the WP site (as blog posts — not a structured section). These need proper pages so they (a) preserve the WP URL equity, (b) rank for "[Brand] authorized dealer [City]" queries, and (c) support the luxury brand narrative.

Architecture:
— src/pages/retailers/index.astro — consolidated dealer locator with map embed, alphabetical list by city, filterable
— src/pages/retailers/[slug].astro — dynamic individual retailer page
— src/content/retailers/[slug].md — one file per dealer with structured frontmatter

Per-retailer frontmatter (minimum):
name, city, state, address, phone, website, hours, description, lat, lng, heroImage

Schema.org on each page: JewelryStore (subtype of LocalBusiness) with name, address, telephone, openingHours, geo, hasMap, brand (Bez Ambar). This is what Google Maps, Bing, Apple Maps, and AI local search all read.

Content per page: Store name + location hero, description, hours, map embed (Google Maps iframe), link to store website, "Authorized Bez Ambar Dealer" badge, CTA to contact Bez Ambar directly

Redirects needed: Each of the ~35 WP retailer post slugs (e.g. /hamra-jewelers-houston) → /retailers/hamra-jewelers-houston

First step: Verify which retailers are still active. The WP posts may be stale — some dealers may have closed or dropped the line. Bez to confirm list before building all 35.
