2026-07-27 · commit c93b249
Home Page Video Preloading — Parallax Carousel Smoothness
Choppy video transitions on the home page parallax stack traced to two root causes: (1) hero vid-0 was set to preload="metadata" — browser loaded dimensions only, video frames had to download from scratch on play; (2) vid1 and cvid1 were never pre-buffered until the exact moment they needed to display. Fixed: vid-0 set to preload="auto"; vid1 starts loading as soon as vid0 begins playing; cvid1 loads alongside cvid0 when the cinematic section enters the viewport. Also added a <link rel="preload"> in <head> for the hero poster frame — first paint now shows the image instantly instead of black.
