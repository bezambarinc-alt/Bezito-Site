Cascata

A sculptural cascade of platinum and light, anchored by a single nine-carat round.

The Design

Some pieces lie quietly on the neck. This one moves.

Three continuous strands of round brilliant-cut diamonds in platinum, each stone graduated, each strand falling at a slightly different length. Where the strands cross, they pass over and under each other like draped ribbons of light — not three parallel lines, but a single woven cascade.

One of the strands does not end at the clasp. It continues past the crossing point, tapering down across the sternum, and resolves in a single nine-carat round brilliant-cut diamond set low in a four-prong platinum basket. The pendant is not a charm hung from a chain. It is the natural terminus of the line — the place where the cascade comes to rest.

Worn, the necklace draws the eye twice: first to the woven strands at the throat, then down to the stillness of the round at the sternum. Two distinct gestures, one continuous piece.
