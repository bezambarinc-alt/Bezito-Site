The Crossover Ashoka®

The Crossover, translated into elongated step-cut light.

Everything in this ring is intentional.

Two rows of Ashoka diamonds. The upper row is big, east to west. The lower row is smaller, north to south, riding a soft wave.

Big and small. East-west and north-south. Wave and still — a single ring with a deliberate division inside it. Not two stackable bands.
