The Calla Ring

A Pear, Held by
Petals of Fire

Thirteen carats. D flawless. Pear-cut, set in platinum. What sets the Calla apart sits beneath the center stone: a ring of marquise-cut diamonds opened like petals around the pavilion. Not a halo. Not a basket.

Each marquise carries its own fire — and passes it upward, into the pear from below. That is why it belongs to the Bloom Collection.

Technical Characteristics

A single pear-cut diamond, set in platinum, oriented along the line of the finger. The Calla takes its name from the calla lily — one petal opening to the light. From the Bloom Collection, where each piece is built around one stone.

13 carats · D color · Flawless clarity · Pear cut · GIA certified.

Platinum 950, hand-finished. Polished exterior, brushed interior. Hallmarked Bez Ambar · Los Angeles.

Set on four hand-shaped platinum prongs — two at the point, two at the shoulders. BEZ engraved on the interior shank by hand. One-of-one construction: no two prongs filed alike.

GIA report for the center stone ships with the piece. Bez Ambar maker's certificate, signed by Bez.
