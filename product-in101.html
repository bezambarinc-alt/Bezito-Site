Baguette Line Necklace

The Baguette Line

A line of baguette diamonds around the neck — step cut, set flat, set forward, one against the next. The rhythm of a baguette in motion, held to the throat at every point. Every necklace is made to fit, measured to the millimetre of the wearer's throat, so that the line runs true from clasp to clasp and does not twist. Designed in Los Angeles by Bez Ambar — the same hand behind the first Princess, forty-seven years on.

The Rhythm of the Baguette

A baguette is the diamond as architecture — step-cut, rectangular, the light held in parallel bars rather than scattered by facets. Set one against the next around the neck, and the piece becomes a line of light with a beat to it: each stone marking a beat, each shared edge marking the next. The Baguette Line is engineered so that every stone sits flat and forward, prong-set at the corners, the shared edges precise enough that the eye reads one continuous stripe of diamond. On the neck, it lies like a necklace. To the eye at a distance, it reads like a line drawn once.

Made to Fit the Neck

Every Baguette Line is measured, calibrated, and hand-fitted to the wearer. The line runs true from clasp to clasp — it does not twist, and it does not gap. That's the guarantee.
