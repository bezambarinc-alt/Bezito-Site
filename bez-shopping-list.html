🛒 Bez's Shopping List

Modified Esselstyn + IBS-D friendly · grouped by store aisle · updated 2026-06-05

🥬 Produce — fresh greens

  • Spinach (your daily workhorse — cooks down fast)
  • Arugula
  • Romaine
  • Swiss chard or kale (for cooking)
  • Parsley (big bunch)

🥕 Produce — vegetables

  • Zucchini
  • Carrots
  • Sweet potato or butternut squash
  • Cucumber
  • Tomato
  • Bell pepper
  • Green beans
  • Green onion tops only (skip white bulbs — high FODMAP)
  • ❌ Avoid raw broccoli / cauliflower / cabbage during flares — cooked only, small portions

🍌 Produce — low-FODMAP fruits

  • Bananas — your daily essential
  • Blueberries / raspberries / strawberries
  • Kiwi
  • Grapes
  • Cantaloupe or honeydew
  • Oranges or grapefruit
  • ❌ Skip: apple, pear, mango, watermelon, dried fruit

🥫 Pantry / dry goods

  • Steel-cut oats (your breakfast workhorse)
  • Brown rice (long-grain or jasmine — cooked soft for IBS gut)
  • Red or orange lentils (gentler than kidney beans)
  • Quinoa
  • Whole-wheat pita OR sourdough whole-grain bread
  • Canned chickpeas + canned black beans — rinse to lower FODMAPs
  • Hemp hearts (sprinkle on oats — protein boost)
  • Hummus (¼ cup serving size for IBS-friendly)
  • Tahini
  • Extra virgin olive oil (1 tbsp/day max — for finishing, not frying)
  • Garlic-infused olive oil (IBS hack — use instead of fresh garlic)
  • Apple cider vinegar / balsamic for salad
  • Coarse unrefined sea salt

🐟 Fish & protein

  • Wild salmon (1–2 fillets — frozen wild is fine)
  • Sardines (1–2 tins — easy fallback days)
  • Sea bass or cod if available
  • Firm tofu (1 block)
  • Tempeh (1 package)
  • Eggs (1 dozen — up to 1/day per updated rule)
  • ❌ Skip: red meat, deli meat, anything processed

🥛 Dairy aisle — mostly skip

  • Unsweetened soy milk (for coffee + oats)
  • Soy yogurt with live cultures (Silk soy yogurt or similar)
  • ❌ Skip: cow's milk, butter, cheese, regular yogurt, cream, sour cream

🌰 Nuts & seeds

  • Walnuts (raw, small handful daily — cardiac + IBS friendly)
  • Pumpkin seeds (for salads)
  • Almonds (optional)

🍫 Treats (allowed)

  • Dark chocolate 70%+ — 1 oz/day allowed
  • Dry red wine (1 glass/night max, 3–4 nights/week)

💊 Supplements / functional

  • Psyllium husk powder (Konsyl plain, NOT Metamucil with sugar) — 1 tsp/day
  • Magnesium glycinate 400 mg — your sleep formula
  • Peppermint oil enteric-coated capsules (Heather's Tummy Tamers or IBgard) — pre-meal for IBS comfort
  • Vitamin D3 2000 IU
  • Align probiotic (Bifidobacterium infantis) — IBS-D specific
Pro move for travel days: grab 2–3 walnut packets + a small bag of plain dried banana chips for the car. Pre-pack snacks = the difference between hitting a Starbucks egg-bite trap and staying on plan.
🚫 Hard skip — don't even walk down these aisles:
  • Bacon, sausage, deli meat
  • Croissants, danish, donuts, bourekas, pastries
  • Anything labeled "creamy" — sauces, dressings, dips
  • Horseradish, wasabi, hot pepper sauces (until gut is fully calm)
  • Fried snacks, chips with hydrogenated oil
  • Sugar-sweetened drinks, fruit juice in volume
