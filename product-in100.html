Only the Line of Diamond

The hardest problem in a thin diamond necklace is the flip. Thin necklaces roll on the neck, and once they roll, only the back of the metal shows — the stones vanish. The Single Row is engineered against that, stone by stone: a linkage built into every join holds each diamond facing forward. Once fitted, it does not turn. The stones stay the side you see.

Every Single Row is measured, engineered, and hand-fitted to the wearer. Once on the neck, it sits upright, and stays that way. That’s the guarantee.
