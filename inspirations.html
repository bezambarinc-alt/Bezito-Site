Section · Rings · Bypass / Toi et Moi

Bypass — two stones, one embrace

The "Toi et Moi" — two stones curved around each other on opposing shanks, no halo, stones carry the drama. Contrast pairs (colored + colorless) are the recurring engine. Two shank languages dominate: bombé pavé all the way around, or tapered/calibré baguettes on the bypass sweep. Cut mixing is deliberate — matched shapes on most, asymmetry lives in color, not shape.
