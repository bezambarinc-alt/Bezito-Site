The 3-of-4 Rule When in Doubt
If a food fits 3 of these 4, it's safe for you:
1. Plant-based (or fish 1–2×/week)
2. Not dairy, not red meat, not fried
3. Cooked (not raw if recovering gut)
4. Not high-FODMAP (no big beans, no raw onion/garlic, no wheat in volume, no high-FODMAP fruit)
