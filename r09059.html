Geometry & Sunlight

Radiant fancy yellows, cradled in a baguette halo. Bez Ambar cuts light itself — geometry and sunlight, fused in platinum and gold.

The Baguette Halo

The radiant cut is a diamond cut that holds its ground. It's not a soft shape — the cropped corners and facet pattern concentrate saturation, turning fancy yellow color deeper and more complete than in almost any other form. These two matched radiants carry 6.90 carats between them, color-matched so that the pair reads as one stone, split across two ears.

Around each stone: a halo of baguettes. Straight-edged, no curves — baguettes don't soften the geometry, they extend it. The rectangular facets of the halo echo the cut of the center stone, pulling the whole composition into a single plane of light. Platinum for the halo, gold beneath — a decision about contrast, not convention.
