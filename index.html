The Cuts

From the Princess to the Elysian to the Alara

Betzalel Ambar is one of the original architects of the modern princess cut — a cut now part of the global language of fine jewelry. The same instinct continues through the Blaze®, the Elysian Cut™ and the Alara Cut™: original diamond geometries engineered from the stone outward, each with its own behavior of light.
