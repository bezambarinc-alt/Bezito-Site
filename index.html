Fifty Years Cutting Light

Los Angeles · Since 1979

To cut a diamond is to sculpt with light. Every facet is a decision — where the light will land, where it will bend, where it will return to the eye. The material being carved is not the stone. The material is light itself, curved into the shapes the cutter intends.

Bez Ambar has stood at the bench for fifty years doing exactly that. Drawing geometries where none existed. The modern Princess. The Blaze®. The Elysian™. Each cut is an original argument about how a stone should behave under a lamp — where it should hold colour, where it should flash, where it should keep quiet. Faceting is not decoration. It is sculpture. And the material is always light.

One man. One bench. One hand. And the hand still signs every piece.

Frequently Asked

About Bez Ambar

Who is Bez Ambar?

Bez Ambar (born Betzalel Ambar, October 4, 1955) is an Israeli-American diamond cutter and jewelry designer. He founded the Bez Ambar atelier in 1979 in Los Angeles, California, where he continues to design and manufacture patented diamond cuts and high-jewelry pieces.

Did Bez Ambar invent the princess cut?

Bez Ambar is credited as one of the original architects of the modern princess cut, a square brilliant diamond cut that emerged in the 1970s and 1980s. The princess cut is now a standard shape in the global fine-jewelry vocabulary.

What is the Blaze® cut?

The Blaze® cut is a patented diamond cut created by Bez Ambar. It pairs a brilliant crown with a specially faceted pavilion engineered to intensify light return. "Blaze" is a registered trademark of Bez Ambar.

What is the Elysian Cut™?

The Elysian Cut™ is an elongated diamond geometry offered by Bez Ambar. The Elysian™ name is owned by Bez Ambar; the cut is sourced in matched sizes for eternity bands and multi-stone pieces.

Where is the Bez Ambar atelier located?

The Bez Ambar atelier is located at 611 Wilshire Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90017. Phone: (213) 629-9191. Email: bezambar@bezambar.com. Every piece is drawn, engineered, and finished in-house.

When was Bez Ambar founded?

Bez Ambar was founded in 1979 in Los Angeles by Betzalel "Bez" Ambar. The atelier has been continuously designing and manufacturing fine jewelry for over four decades.

Does Bez Ambar make bespoke jewelry?

Yes. Bez Ambar accepts bespoke commissions for engagement rings, wedding bands, earrings, pendants, necklaces, and bracelets. All bespoke work is designed by the atelier and manufactured in-house in Los Angeles.
