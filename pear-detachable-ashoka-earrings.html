Pear & Detachable Ashoka

A pear-shaped diamond stud. Suspended from it, an Ashoka-cut diamond drop framed in a double row of pavé. The bail locks so the drop cannot fall — but the Ashoka moves freely, catching light with every turn of the head. The motion is the point. Platinum.

The Connection

We are currently developing two approaches to attach the Ashoka drop to the pear stud.

Option A — Locking bail. A precision hook seats into a channel at the back of the pear’s setting and locks in place once the drop is hanging. The Ashoka swings freely — the movement catches light — but cannot disengage during wear. Currently in wear-testing.

Option B — Post-through drop. The drop attaches via a pin that passes through the center of the pear-shaped frame, stepped to sit flush against the bezel. Currently in fabrication.

Both options will be available for evaluation once testing is complete. We will share results and recommend the preferred mechanism before the piece goes into production.

In Development

The connection mechanism for this piece is currently being tested. Bez Ambar will not release a piece until the security of the attachment is confirmed through wear-testing. The Ashoka will not release in wear — that standard applies here as with every piece we make.
