The Step Cut

An Emerald Cut does not scatter light the way a brilliant does. Its long parallel facets act as mirrors — they reflect the environment in broad flashes rather than the rapid pin-fire of a round brilliant, and at 10.50 carats the surface area is large enough for those reflections to become deep, shifting planes of white and black. E color in a step cut means the stone reads as absolute colorless; any warmth would show in those large table facets in a way it cannot hide. The Cadillac-cut side stones — D color, one VS1, one VVS2 — extend that geometry laterally, their pointed kite shapes bridging the gap between the rectangular center and the round shank.
