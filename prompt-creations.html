Style · Design Presentation

Stone Sketch — atelier presentation style

Stone Sketch is the named presentation style for design-phase and hero-card imagery. Real stones sitting on a loose freehand pencil sketch of the ring — deep mid-gray paper, single hard side key from upper left, dramatic diagonal shadows that glow with each stone's own color inside the darkness. Van Cleef "Pierres de Caractère" spirit. Fully universal: swap the ring design and the featured stones per shot.

Style · Stone Sketch · Universal

Stone Sketch

Deep Gray Paper Side Key Color-in-Shadow Freehand Sketch Universal

Universal template. Fill two placeholders — the ring design and which stones are real — and the invariants (paper, light, shadow-glow, freehand line) do the rest. Any stone color, any silhouette. Accent stones stay drawn.

Prompt 
Photorealistic jewelry atelier presentation in the "STONE SKETCH" style. Background: DEEP MID-GRAY paper (moody neutral gray, roughly 40% gray, cool, tending toward charcoal at the corners) filling the entire frame, visible paper tooth and cotton grain, deep atmospheric falloff so the edges read almost black. Lighting: strong single directional key from the upper left, warm and focused, creating dramatic long diagonal shadows to the lower right — high contrast, moody, cinematic. Beneath and around the stones: a LOOSE FREEHAND graphite pencil sketch of the ring design described here — [RING DESIGN DESCRIPTION: silhouette, gallery, prongs, shank, accent stones and their placement]. Sketch style: free hand designer's quick line, confident but not precise, uneven pressure, some lines doubled or ghosted, construction marks visible, real hand-drawn energy — NOT drafted, NOT ruler-straight, NOT a technical drawing. Graphite reads clearly against the deep gray paper. On top of the sketch, sitting as real physical stones on the paper: [STONE COUNT AND DESCRIPTION: e.g. "two loose fancy vivid yellow pear-shape diamonds" / "three loose Colombian emerald step-cuts" / "one 5ct oval Burmese ruby flanked by two D-color trapezoid diamonds"], oriented exactly as in the reference design, high internal brilliance, sharp facet reflections. From each real stone a LONG DRAMATIC DIAGONAL SHADOW stretches across the paper to the lower right (roughly 2× to 3× the stone's length), and INSIDE each shadow THE STONE'S OWN COLOR bleeds through as a luminous glow — yellow diamonds → warm canary glow inside the shadow; emeralds → deep green glow; rubies → warm red glow; sapphires → deep blue glow; white diamonds → cool prismatic bounce. This color-in-shadow effect is the Stone Sketch signature: color inside the darkness, not on the paper around the stone. Any accent stones in the design that are NOT called out as real remain as loose pencil sketch only — drawn, not real. Sharp focus on the real stones and their glowing shadows, softer on the pencil work. Cinematic haute joaillerie campaign in the Van Cleef & Arpels "Pierres de Caractère" spirit.
(End of prompt — 2026-07-04)

Bulgari Sketch

Black Paper Gouache Hero Sketch Alternates Construction Lines Blind Emboss Universal

Universal template. Fill one placeholder — the featured piece — and the invariants (black paper, gouache render, sketch alternates + construction lines, BEZ AMBAR blind emboss) do the rest. No writing anywhere; only linework and the emboss.

Prompt 
Photorealistic Bez Ambar atelier design plate in the classic maison archival style. Background: deep charcoal / near-black textured paper (~15% gray, matte, subtle paper grain and fiber visible) filling the entire frame — the drama comes from the darkness. Composition is a TWO-LAYER ATELIER DESIGN PLATE:

LAYER 1 — the featured piece (HERO): a photorealistic rendered reproduction of the exact jewelry piece shown in the reference image — faithfully capturing its silhouette, stone arrangement, cut shapes, metal details, and prong style — composed slightly above center, RENDERED IN RICH PAINTERLY OIL COLORS WITH PHOTOGRAPHIC-LEVEL DETAIL — luminous, dimensional, deep saturation, sharp facet definition, dramatic highlight-to-shadow contrast, subtle impasto brushwork on the metal, hyperreal quality that makes the piece look almost tangible against the black. The featured stones are rendered in their TRUE COLORS as shown in the reference image — do NOT invent stone colors not present in the reference. If the reference shows white diamonds, render them as brilliant icy-white diamonds with no color tint. If it shows colored stones, render those exact colors only. Metalwork rendered with painterly white and pale-gray impasto suggesting brilliance and dimensionality. The featured piece OWNS the composition's attention. Approximately 50–60% of the frame.

LAYER 2 — SUBDUED pencil sketch alternates and construction lines in the surrounding negative space (upper corners, lower corners, and along the edges): 3–5 small quick freehand pencil-sketch variations of the same design DRAWN IN SOFT WHITE / LIGHT GRAY LINE (luminous white pencil on the dark paper, high contrast — the classic jeweler's sketch-on-black-board quality; lines read clearly bright against the deep charcoal) — alternate silhouettes, alternate gallery treatments, alternate prong studies, alternate profile views. Include visible CONSTRUCTION LINES: light guide axes, centerline crosses, circle-of-symmetry marks, radial construction arcs, faint dimension marks — also in the same soft white pencil line. Cross-hatched shading on the sketches, quick unfinished energy, real hand-drawn feel — NOT drafted, NOT ruler-perfect. The sketches are SECONDARY information, ambient design context — the eye should go to the hero piece first and only. NO handwritten text, NO annotations, NO labels, NO numbers, NO letters — only linework.

BLIND EMBOSS: in the LOWER RIGHT corner, a small round BLIND EMBOSS pressed directly into the charcoal paper (NO INK, NO COLOR, NO WAX — just relief in the paper itself). Roughly 2cm diameter. The emboss reads "BEZ AMBAR" in elegant serif around the perimeter with a small crown or diamond emblem at center. The emboss is DEBOSSED (pressed inward) with a subtle raised inner surface, catching a soft highlight along the upper-left edge and a delicate shadow along the lower-right edge from the key light — visible only through subtle relief, not through pigment. Elegant, restrained, Patek-level quiet.

Overall mood: fine art archival design plate, classic haute joaillerie atelier heritage, dignified, reverent — the way a private commission would be blocked out in a leather-bound design book. Sharp focus on the rendered piece, softer on the sketches. NO Bulgari markings, NO Cartier markings, NO other house names, NO writing anywhere — only linework and the BEZ AMBAR blind emboss.
(End of prompt — 2026-07-04)

Style · Graff Drawing · Universal

Graff Drawing

Cream Paper Three Views Watercolor + Pencil Copperplate Annotations BEZ AMBAR Stamp Universal

Universal template. Fill two placeholders — the piece + the annotations — and the invariants (cream paper, three views, translucent watercolor + fine pencil, copperplate script, subtle stamp) do the rest. Quiet, refined, atelier-precise.

Prompt 
Photorealistic Bez Ambar atelier design drawing in the classic "GRAFF DRAWING" style. Background: bright warm off-white cotton watercolor paper (soft cream, subtle warmth, roughly 92% white) filling the entire frame, visible watercolor tooth and cotton grain, light diffuse overhead lighting, corners very softly darker with a delicate vignette — luminous, refined, restrained.

Composition is a VERTICAL MULTI-VIEW DESIGN PLATE showing the same piece from three angles stacked top to bottom, each centered on the vertical midline:
- TOP: profile / side view of [PIECE DESCRIPTION]
- MIDDLE: front-facing hero view showing the featured stone(s) full face
- BOTTOM: three-quarter or dimensional view showing depth

Behind each view: very faint pencil centerline crosses and light construction axes in barely-visible graphite (~15% gray, ghosted, ambient), suggesting the geometric setup. Roughly equal spacing between the three views, generous breathing room.

RENDERING STYLE — delicate watercolor + fine pencil, illustrator-quality:
- Metal (white gold, platinum, yellow gold): translucent light gray or gold watercolor washes with soft graphite linework defining edges. Refined, gentle, never heavy.
- Featured colored stones: saturated but delicate watercolor washes in the stone's true color — yellow diamonds in warm luminous canary yellow with visible facet lines drawn in fine pencil; emeralds in cool clear green; rubies in warm red; sapphires in cornflower blue. All rendered with translucency and internal light, not opaque paint. The paper glows through.
- Accent white diamonds: pale translucent gray-blue washes with faint pencil facet lines.
- Overall: SOFT, LUMINOUS, REFINED — as if the artist used minimal pigment and let the paper breathe.

HANDWRITTEN ANNOTATIONS: elegant copperplate script in soft graphite gray (~40% gray), positioned in the negative space to the LEFT and RIGHT of the views, pointing to specific elements with delicate dotted leader lines. Written in an atelier hand — beautiful, slightly imperfect, atelier-authentic. Annotations for this piece: [ANNOTATIONS — e.g. "Brilliant cut white diamond shoulders" / "16ct fancy intense radiant cut Yellow diamond" / "Platinum shank" / "Fancy vivid yellow pear diamonds 8ct total"]. Breathing room preserved — annotations don't crowd the piece.

BEZ AMBAR STAMP: small circular stamp in the LOWER RIGHT corner — LIGHT GRAY, LOW CONTRAST, printed feel (not embossed, not wax). Reads "DESIGN · BEZ AMBAR · WORLDWIDE" in fine serif letters around the circumference with a small emblem at center. Roughly 2cm diameter. Delicate, understated, atelier signature — visible but quiet.

Overall mood: fine art atelier design drawing, refined and restrained, private-commission luxury, the way a top-house design would be presented in a leather portfolio to a client. NO Graff markings, NO Cartier markings, NO other house names — only BEZ AMBAR.

Variant · 16:9 Landscape — Gouache, No Writing
Prompt — Landscape 
Photorealistic Bez Ambar atelier design drawing in the classic "GRAFF DRAWING" style, LANDSCAPE 16:9 aspect ratio. Background: bright warm off-white cotton paper (soft cream, subtle warmth, roughly 92% white) filling the entire frame, visible fine paper grain, light diffuse overhead lighting, corners very softly darker with a delicate vignette — luminous, refined.

Composition is a HORIZONTAL MULTI-VIEW DESIGN PLATE showing the same piece from three angles arranged LEFT to RIGHT, each centered on the horizontal midline and aligned along a common baseline:
- LEFT: profile / side view of [PIECE DESCRIPTION]
- CENTER: front-facing hero view showing the featured stone(s) full face
- RIGHT: three-quarter or dimensional view showing depth

CONSTRUCTION LINES — visible and expressive on the bare paper around the piece: pencil centerline crosses and light construction axes behind each view in soft graphite (~25% gray), radiating axis lines, circle-of-symmetry marks, faint radial guides, gentle triangulation lines. NO dimension numbers, NO measurements, NO handwritten labels — just the pencil geometry.

RENDERING STYLE — OPAQUE GOUACHE PAINTING WITH PHOTOREALISTIC DETAIL, by a master illustrator's hand. NOT translucent watercolor — solid pigment coverage, dimensional impasto quality, painterly brushwork combined with photographic-level realism in every facet, prong, and reflection. Every surface built up from layered gouache pigments: WIDE MULTI-PIGMENT PALETTE, at least 10–12 distinct color notes visible across the piece rendering. Never one flat color. Every surface — metal and stone — layered from multiple pigments the way a master gouache artist works: not a single yellow but yellow + amber + warm gold + cool green shadow + pink highlight + violet-blue pavilion + coral bounce; not a single red but red + rose + violet-red + orange-red + pink edge + blue undertone. Every stone is a symphony of tones, painted with photographic precision.

METAL PALETTE (per view, ~5–6 notes visible), painted in opaque gouache:
- Deep graphite / near-black line defining the edges and prongs
- Cool blue-gray in the recesses and shadows
- Warm gold-touched highlight where light catches the crest
- Silver-white crest reflections, opaque and bright
- Warm coral or pink BOUNCE tones from neighboring colored stones reflected into the metal surface
- Cool green or blue COMPLEMENTARY-COLOR shadows in the deepest folds
- Painterly brushwork visible in the strokes, but every facet edge photographically sharp

STONE PALETTE (per stone, ~7–8 notes visible in solid layered gouache):
- Core saturation in the stone's true color, deep and rich
- A warmer neighbor tone (yellow → warm orange-amber; ruby → warm rose; sapphire → warm violet-purple; emerald → warm olive)
- A cooler complementary shadow tone in the pavilion (yellow → cool green; ruby → cool blue; sapphire → deep indigo; emerald → cool teal)
- A bright pigment highlight on the crown catching the light — opaque, luminous, painted with a small precise brush
- A darker culet accent at the point
- A metallic bounce (gold, rose, or silver) reflecting from the setting
- Fine facet linework cross-hatching the interior structure with photographic precision
- A subtle secondary refraction color inside the belly (pink, coral, or amethyst flash)
- SUPER REALISTIC facet definition — individual crown facets, table edges, pavilion star facets all clearly resolved

WHITE ACCENT DIAMONDS — layered pale gray-blue gouache with individually painted facet edges, warm coral and pink bounce tones from neighboring colored stones, subtle prismatic rainbow flashes inside the facets, opaque and dimensional.

Overall the piece should carry AT LEAST 10–12 DISTINCT COLOR NOTES visible when the eye scans across the rendering, executed in OPAQUE GOUACHE with painterly brushwork BUT photorealistic facet-level detail. The paper is the ground around the piece; the piece itself is painted SOLID and DIMENSIONAL, not translucent. Warm and cool tones playing against each other, every surface catching secondary colors from every other surface, the whole thing luminous and hyperreal.

BEZ AMBAR STAMP: small circular stamp in the LOWER RIGHT corner — light gray, low contrast, printed feel (not embossed, not wax). Reads "DESIGN · BEZ AMBAR · WORLDWIDE" in fine serif letters around the circumference with a small emblem at center. Roughly 2cm diameter. Delicate, understated.

Overall mood: fine art atelier design drawing by a MASTER GOUACHE ILLUSTRATOR — richly colored, painterly, photorealistic, dimensional. NO handwritten text anywhere. NO annotations. NO labels. NO writing of any kind — only construction lines, the piece, and the stamp. NO Graff markings, NO Cartier markings, NO other house names — only BEZ AMBAR. Final image aspect: 16:9 landscape, three views spanning the horizontal length.
(End of prompt — 2026-07-04)

Tool · Universal · Any Diamond Piece

Diamond Enhancer

Facet Sharpening Dot Artifact Fix Selection Pass Run 2× if Needed

Select only the diamond/jewelry area — not skin or background. Paste this prompt into GenFill or your inpainting tool. The geometric plane language forces the model to resolve hard facet edges instead of averaging into noise. Run a second pass on the same selection if dots remain.

Prompt 
Ultra-sharp photorealistic diamond facets. Each facet is a flat geometric plane — table, crown facets, star facets, upper girdle facets, pavilion — separated by razor-crisp edges with no blurring, no softening, no pixelation. Where two facets meet, the edge is a hard line. The interior of each facet is a single clean mirror reflection: either a bright specular highlight, a cool blue-gray depth tone, or a white prismatic flash — not blended together, not blurry, not dotted. No scattered sparkle blobs. No "glitter" noise. Diamond brilliance comes from the sharp contrast between adjacent facets, each reading its own clean flat tone. Photorealistic luxury jewelry photography, shot at f/8, studio strobe, medium format. The result should look like a GIA grading photograph, not an AI render.
