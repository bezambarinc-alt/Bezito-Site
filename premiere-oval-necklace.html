The Première

A 25-carat oval diamond anchors the center, its elongated form set at the lowest point of the necklace's V-shaped cascade; radiating outward from it, baguette-cut diamonds arranged in graduated tiers — each stone angled inward to follow the collarbone — are framed at the perimeter by Ashoka and square-cut diamonds whose broad, open facets amplify the light moving through the entire piece. Set in 18K white gold or platinum.

The Center Oval

The 25-carat oval is chosen for its surface area — more face-up brilliance per carat than any other fancy shape — and for the way its curved girdle softens the formality of the geometric baguette work around it. It is the piece's only curved element; everything else is straight-edged, angular, architectural. That single curve is what makes the necklace move.

Made for the Wearer

Every Bez Ambar necklace is sized and finished to the wearer's throat before delivery. No exceptions.
