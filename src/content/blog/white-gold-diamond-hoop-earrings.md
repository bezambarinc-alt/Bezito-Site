*White gold diamond hoops are the most versatile fine jewelry earring in production. The reasons are structural, not sentimental.*

---

A white gold diamond hoop earring does one specific thing exceptionally well: it provides continuous, uninterrupted light return across a circular form that moves with the wearer. The motion of the hoop as the head turns catches light from multiple directions simultaneously, which is why diamond hoops under any light source produce a quality of sparkle that studs, which face only one direction, cannot match.

This is not a small distinction. It is why diamond hoops have remained in continuous production across every decade of modern jewelry design, while other earring forms cycle in and out of fashion.

### White Gold
## vs Platinum

Both white gold and platinum read as white metals in finished jewelry, but they are different materials with different properties.

White gold is yellow gold alloyed with palladium or silver to neutralize the yellow color, then finished with a layer of rhodium plating to achieve the bright white surface. Over time, that rhodium layer wears off in areas of friction, revealing a slightly warmer underlying color. Most white gold earrings need replating every two to five years depending on wear and body chemistry.

Platinum is naturally white and does not require plating. It develops a patina over time where the surface scratches and burnishes, creating a slightly satin finish. Many people prefer this patina; others prefer the original mirror finish, which can be restored by polishing. Platinum is denser and heavier than gold, and the per-gram cost is higher.

For hoop earrings, white gold is the practical choice for most buyers. The replating cost is modest and the weight savings versus platinum is meaningful for hoops worn daily. Platinum is the right choice for buyers who want the longevity of a material that never needs replating and do not mind the slightly heavier feel.

### Inside Set vs Outside Set
## Diamond Placement

Diamond hoops are set in two basic configurations.

Outside set hoops have diamonds on the exterior surface of the hoop as it faces the world. This configuration is immediately visible and creates a ring of visible light around the circular form. It reads from a distance and works well in large hoops.

Inside set hoops have diamonds on the inner curved surface of the hoop, the side facing the neck and face. These diamonds are not directly visible from the front but create an effect where light seems to come from inside the hoop as the earring moves. The effect is more subtle and intimate.

Eternity hoops, set fully around the circumference, combine both effects. They are heavier and more expensive, but they produce the most complete light return and read from every angle.

### Diameter
## and Weight

Hoop diameter ranges from small huggie hoops (10-15mm, hugging the lobe) to substantial statement hoops (35-50mm or more). The right diameter depends on two factors: how prominent you want the earring to be, and what the earring weighs.

Larger hoops in precious metal and diamonds become heavy. A 40mm hoop in 18k gold with full diamond setting is a different weight proposition than a 20mm hoop in the same materials. For daily wear, most people find hoops in the 20-30mm range to be comfortable across long days.

Gauge, the thickness of the hoop itself, also affects weight and visual presence. A heavier gauge hoop in the 15-20mm diameter can look more substantial than a thinner-gauge hoop of 30mm. The combination of diameter and gauge determines both the silhouette and the weight.

### What People
## Ask Us

**How often does white gold need to be rhodium plated?**
Typically every two to five years for earrings, depending on wear and individual body chemistry. Some people's skin chemistry wears rhodium faster than others. The replating process is straightforward for any jeweler and is not expensive. When the underlying warm color begins to show through in worn areas, it is time to replate.

**Are white gold hoops appropriate for everyday wear?**
Yes, well-made white gold hoops are designed for daily wear. Secure the closure correctly each time (click closures, lever backs, or hinged closures are most secure), and inspect the stone settings every year or two to ensure prongs or pavé settings have not loosened.

**What diameter hoop works for most people?**
For daily wear, 20 to 30mm covers most people comfortably. Below 15mm reads as a huggie. Above 35mm becomes a statement piece that many people reserve for evenings or occasions when the earring can be the focal point. The right size depends on the scale of your features and how visible you want the earrings to be.

**Do diamond hoops work with other jewelry?**
Yes. White gold diamond hoops are among the most compatible earrings with other fine jewelry because their neutral metal and white diamond light do not compete with other colors or forms. They work with bracelets, necklaces, and rings of varying designs without creating visual conflict.

**What is the difference between Bez Ambar's Light Hoops and standard diamond hoops?**
The Light Hoops use Blaze® accent stones on the inner facing of the hoop rather than standard round pavé diamonds. The Blaze® stones, being custom-cut and edge-to-edge, create a more continuous inner ring of light than standard pavé, which has visible gaps between stones. The effect from the front is a hoop that reads as more solidly set with light.
