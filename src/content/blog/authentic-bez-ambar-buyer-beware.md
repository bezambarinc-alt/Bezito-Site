### The Brand in Brief
## Three Decades of Studio Production

Bez Ambar has been manufacturing fine diamond jewelry in Los Angeles since the early 1980s. The studio is in Downtown LA, a few blocks from the Los Angeles diamond district. Every piece, engagement rings, wedding bands, pendants, earrings, bracelets, is made in that building.

The studio is not a large-scale manufacturer. Production volume is deliberately limited in favor of quality. Individual pieces receive attention from skilled bench jewelers who have worked with Bez for years, in many cases decades.

The Bez Ambar collection includes pieces with proprietary diamond cuts, the Blaze® (registered trademark) and the Quadrillion Cut™, that are cut in-house to specifications developed over Bez's career. These cuts cannot be replicated by manufacturers sourcing commodity diamonds, because the facet geometry requires cutting to tolerances that mass production cannot sustain.

### How Authentic Pieces Are Sold
## Authorized Channels Only

Bez Ambar pieces reach customers through two channels: authorized retail partners and direct purchase from the studio.

**Authorized retailers** are independent fine jewelry stores that carry Bez Ambar as part of their curated selection. These stores have a direct relationship with the studio, they purchase pieces through the studio, provide proper product information to customers, and can speak to the provenance of any Bez Ambar piece they carry. A list of authorized retailers is available through the studio.

**Direct from the studio** means buying from Bez Ambar directly, through the studio's website or by visiting the Downtown Los Angeles location. Custom work is typically handled through the studio directly.

Bez Ambar does not wholesale through general jewelry distribution channels. A piece appearing at a retailer that has no established relationship with the studio is a reason for scrutiny.

### Red Flags
## What to Watch For

**Third-party marketplaces.** Bez Ambar does not sell through eBay, Amazon, Etsy, or similar platforms. A listing on these platforms may represent a secondhand piece (which could be genuine) or may represent a misidentified or falsely described piece. In either case, you are buying without the provenance documentation that comes from an authorized channel.

**Prices significantly below retail.** Bez Ambar pricing reflects the cost of studio production, in-house cutting, skilled bench work, quality materials. A price 40% below what any authorized retailer carries the same piece for should prompt questions. Authentic pieces from authorized retailers are priced consistently because they carry the same costs.

**Vague or missing provenance.** A genuine Bez Ambar piece sold by an authorized retailer will come with documentation: a purchase receipt identifying it as a Bez Ambar piece, typically with a model number or custom reference. Sellers who cannot provide a clear chain of purchase, who acquired it and from where, are selling without the documentation that authenticates a piece.

**Generic descriptions.** Legitimate sellers of Bez Ambar jewelry know what they are selling. If a seller describes the piece vaguely ("nice diamond ring, may be Bez Ambar"), they likely do not know its provenance.

### If You Already Own a Piece
## How to Verify Authenticity

If you purchased a Bez Ambar piece secondhand and want to verify its authenticity, contact the studio directly. Bring or send photographs of the piece, including close-up images of any hallmarks or engravings. The studio can often confirm whether a piece matches their production records based on design details.

Most Bez Ambar pieces carry hallmarks identifying the metal content and, in many cases, the studio. These are not foolproof authentication, hallmarks can be counterfeited, but they are a starting point.

For high-value pieces, a formal appraisal from an independent GIA-certified gemologist who is familiar with Bez Ambar's work will provide documentation useful for insurance purposes and resale.

### Working with the Studio
## Custom and Direct Purchase

The most reliable way to ensure you are getting a genuine Bez Ambar piece is to work directly with the studio, either through a visit to the Downtown Los Angeles location or through the website.

Custom work is a significant part of what the studio does. If you have a design in mind, a specific diamond shape, a setting style, a metal combination, the studio can produce it. Custom pieces come with full documentation of materials, specifications, and provenance.

The studio also carries ready-to-purchase pieces from the current collection for clients who prefer selecting from finished work rather than commissioning from scratch.

Every Bez Ambar piece is made in the Downtown Los Angeles studio. This is not production outsourced to overseas manufacturing, the bench jewelers, the diamond cutters, and Bez himself work in the same building.

Authentic pieces are sold through authorized retail partners and directly through the Bez Ambar studio. Pieces purchased through these channels come with proper documentation. No. Bez Ambar does not sell through eBay, Amazon, Etsy, or similar third-party marketplaces. Listings on these platforms represent either secondhand pieces (which may be genuine) or misidentified items. Buy from authorized channels for guaranteed provenance.

**What are the authorized retailers for Bez Ambar jewelry?**
The network of authorized retailers includes fine jewelry stores across the United States that have a direct purchasing relationship with the Bez Ambar studio. Contact the studio directly for a current list of authorized retailers in your area.

**Can I have a custom Bez Ambar piece made?**
Yes. Custom work is a core part of what the studio does. Contact the studio to discuss your design ideas, budget, timeline, and specifications. Custom pieces are produced entirely in-house and come with complete documentation of materials and provenance.
