The Elysian Cut™

A Family of Cuts

An old idea — the step cut, centuries in the making — reborn across seven geometries.

The Elysian Cut™ is not a single shape. It is a family of original step-cut geometries, each engineered from the stone outward: the line of the crown, the relationship of the pavilion, the angles that decide whether light becomes a flicker or a continuous line.

Each geometry is original — patented or patent pending. Each is made for calibration — the kind of stone-to-stone precision that lets a band or a necklace behave as one piece of light, not a row of independent stones.
