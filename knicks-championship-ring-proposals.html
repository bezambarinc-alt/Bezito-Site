Concept Study · 2026
New York Knicks

Championship Ring Proposals

There is one cut of diamond that belongs to New York. The Ashoka® — William Goldberg's elongated cushion, born in 1956 in a workshop on Fifth Avenue — has never been made anywhere else with the same precision. Setting them around the perimeter of a championship ring is the closest a wearer can come to wearing the city itself. From there, every layer earns its place: fancy-orange Carré gemstones for KNICKS in rose gold, a Carré-tiled basketball at the heart, Madison Square Garden under a hinged crown, the Statue of Liberty on the gallery, the player's name inside the shank. This is not a ring decorated with New York symbols. It is New York, built into a piece of jewelry.

Design Elements
  • Ashoka® cut diamonds — perimeter
  • Fancy-orange Carré gemstones — KNICKS lettering
  • Carré-tiled basketball — center
  • Rose gold — orange gemstone settings
  • Madison Square Garden — hinged crown
  • Statue of Liberty — side gallery
  • Blue sapphires — inner frame
  • Player name engraving — inner shank
