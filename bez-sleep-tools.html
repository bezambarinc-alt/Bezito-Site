🛏 Back-to-sleep protocol (5 AM waking)

  1. Phone face-down, don't look at it again. Blue light kills melatonin.
  2. Bathroom if needed, back in bed.
  3. Cool the room (AC down 2°F or crack a window).
  4. 4–7–8 breathing × 4 cycles. In 4, hold 7, out 8.
  5. Yoga Nidra on (link above) — volume just-loud-enough, headphones if you have them.
  6. Lie on your back, palms up, feet falling open. Don't try to fall asleep. Aim to rest calmly.
  7. If still awake at 5:25 AM → get up briefly, read a paper book in dim warm light, then return.

🌅 If you don't get back to sleep

  • 20-min nap between 1–2 PM today (cleanest recovery)
  • Skip heavy coffee — one early small cup max
  • Magnesium glycinate 400 mg + brown noise tonight, in bed by 10:30 PM
  • Don't write off the day — sleep debt recovers within 24 hours of one good night
