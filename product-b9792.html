B9792

Camélia
Bloom Collection

Twenty-Five Carats. Zero Bowtie.

The bowtie is the cutter's signature scar on an oval — the dark band that crosses the long axis when the rough is yielded at any cost. The Camélia carries none. Twenty-five carats, D color, VS2 clarity, cut to a single plan: light enters at the crown, returns at the crown, leaves no shadow behind. From the Bloom Collection, where each piece is built around one stone.

The Design

Fancy intense pink diamonds run the length of the wire — around the oval as a halo, down the split shank, framing the colorless heart in pink fire. The shank lifts the oval clear of the finger, and the stone reads as suspended — full side profile of the facets visible from any angle. Set in rose gold to carry the pink down through the metal.

The Setting

Pink-pavé halo and split shank, rose gold, hand-set.

Details

Technical Characteristics

A 25-carat oval, D color, VS2 clarity, cut without bowtie. A halo of fancy intense pink diamonds frames the stone; the same pavé runs the split shank down the wire. The oval reads as suspended above the finger, with the full side profile of its facets in view. From the Bloom Collection.

Center: 25 carats · D color · VS2 clarity · Oval brilliant cut · No bowtie · GIA certified.

Pavé: Natural fancy intense pink diamonds, hand-matched, set across the halo and the full length of the split shank.

18-karat rose gold, hand-finished. Polished exterior, brushed interior. Hallmarked Bez Ambar · Los Angeles.

Split-shank construction, each shoulder hand-shaped. Pink diamonds set individually across halo and shank. BEZ engraved on the interior shank by hand. One-of-one piece.

GIA report for the center stone ships with the piece. Bez Ambar maker's certificate, signed by Bez.
