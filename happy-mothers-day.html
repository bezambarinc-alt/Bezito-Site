Happy Mother's Day

Celebrating the women who carried us — and the love that finds its way into something you can hold.

In 1992 — thirty‑four years ago — we set out to make a piece for the love between a child and a mother. Rubies of the deepest, most honest red — recut to fit each other invisibly, stone touching stone — arranged into the unbroken shape of a heart. Around it, a frame of small brilliant‑cut diamonds: a halo, but quieter.

It became a classic. Today it lives in our archive. On this Mother's Day, we wanted to share it with you.

— With love, from the Bez Ambar family

The Stone

A Single, Honest Red

Each ruby was chosen for the depth of its color and recut to fit Bez Ambar's proprietary heart-shape configuration. Set invisibly — stone touching stone — they form a seamless continuum: a unity of color that lives beyond the limitation of a single gem.

The Frame

Quiet Brilliance

Around the heart, a halo of small brilliant-cut diamonds. Not loud, not announced — placed to lift the ruby and follow its shape. The diamonds are there to celebrate the heart, not to compete with it.

The Story

From the Archive

Designed in 1992 — thirty‑four years ago. It became a classic — a piece that the family kept making, again and again, because every mother who saw it understood it. Today it sits in our archive, worn into our story. We wanted to share it with you on this day.

"To every mom who is loved, who is missed, who is celebrated today — we wish you a beautiful Mother's Day."
