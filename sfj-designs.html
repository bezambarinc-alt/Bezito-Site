Inverted Jenny
All-Diamond Necklace · Platinum · $10M · In Design

The rarest stamp in American philately — the Inverted Jenny, a 24-cent 1918 airmail stamp with an upside-down biplane — set as the heart of a $10M high-jewelry necklace. The stamp (25×29mm) anchors a 55×59mm pendant: 28 Blaze® squares (4mm) form the inner ring, 20 round brilliants (7.4mm · ~1.5ct each) the outer halo. A 12mm round brilliant bail (~6.5ct) feeds an alternating chain of 18 emerald-cut (11×7.6mm · ~2.5ct) and 18 round (9mm · ~2.7ct) diamonds — 86 stones total. Every stone D/VS1, platinum-set.

Tessera
Emerald · Diamond · Platinum · Design Exploration

A geometric high-jewelry necklace centered on a vivid emerald-cut emerald, framed by a stepped octagonal halo of trapezoid-cut diamonds radiating outward in a tessellation pattern.
