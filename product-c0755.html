Single Row Asscher Cut

Single Row Asscher Cut

A single line of Asscher — quarter carats laid corner to corner, each stone stepped and squared, the whole line reading as one continuous architecture of step-cut light. Every stone G colour, every stone VS clarity, every stone cut to the same millimetre; each anchored in the Bez Ambar Flex mechanism so the bracelet drapes and moves as fabric on the wrist, returning to its line when the hand comes to rest. The Asscher is the step-cut at its most disciplined — cropped corners, wide open table, concentric planes — and it reads truest set one row deep. The same principle Bez Ambar has held since 1979, the same hand, forty-seven years on.

Architecture, One Row Deep

The Asscher is architecture. Its cropped corners, its wide open table, its concentric step facets — the cut is engineered for geometry, not for flash. Set them in a single row, laid corner to corner and matched to the same weight, and what runs down the line is one continuous plane of step-cut light. That is why we set this piece one row deep. The Flex mechanism carries it around the wrist without breaking the line.

It Moves Like Fabric

Every Single Row Asscher is built on the Bez Ambar Flex mechanism. It drapes to the wrist, moves with the hand, and returns to its shape. That's the guarantee.

Technical Details

Gem Stones

Asscher cut, quarter carat each, set in a single row corner to corner. Clarity VS. Colour G.

Metal

Available in 18K white gold, yellow gold, or rose gold. Also in platinum.

Size

Made to fit. From 6.5 to 8.5 inches — measured to the wearer's wrist.

More Details

Built on the Bez Ambar Flex mechanism. Hidden linkage between every set of stones, hidden box clasp. The bracelet drapes to the wrist and returns to its shape.
