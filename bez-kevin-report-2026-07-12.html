Freshsales — Rename Deal Stages to Full Jewelry Pipeline
Freshsales ships with generic CRM stage names (New, Qualification, Discovery, Demo, Negotiation, Won, Lost). These don't map to how a bespoke jewelry atelier actually works — they're SaaS-sales language, not jewelry-sales language. Rename them to reflect the real customer journey across all channels: website inquiries, commissions, collection sales, walk-ins, repairs.

Where to do it:
bezambar.myfreshworks.com → Admin (gear icon, top-right) → CRM → Deal Stages → click pencil on each row.

Rename map:
Current (default) Rename to What it means Applies to
New Inquiry Received First contact — any source: web form, referral, walk-in, event, phone All channels
Qualification Consultation Scheduled Appointment booked — in-person or video call All channels
Discovery Consultation Complete Met with client — needs understood, direction set, next step agreed Commission, bespoke
Demo Design Phase Active bespoke design in progress — CADs, stone selection, revisions Commission only
Negotiation Quote Sent Pricing proposal delivered to client — awaiting decision All channels
— Deposit Received (add new) Client committed — deposit paid, piece confirmed in production queue Commission, collection
— In Production (add new) Piece being made or stone being sourced Commission, bespoke, some collection
— Ready for Delivery (add new) Piece complete, QC passed — awaiting pickup or shipping All channels
Won Sale Complete Delivered and paid in full All channels
Lost Lost (keep as-is) Dead deal — no purchase All channels
— On Hold (add new) Client paused — budget timing, life event, etc. Keep warm, follow up in 60–90 days All channels

Why this matters: Every time Bezito creates a deal from a web inquiry, it gets tagged "Inquiry Received" — which maps exactly to the worker's FS_DEAL_STAGE_INQUIRY constant. The stages above are just renamed display labels; the underlying IDs stay the same. Nothing in the code breaks. But now the pipeline makes sense to Bez, Kevin, and any future team member looking at the board.

Three stages to ADD (not just rename): Deposit Received, In Production, Ready for Delivery. These don't exist in the default Freshsales setup. Add them via the same Deal Stages admin screen — there's an "+ Add Stage" button.

Floating inquiry pill — IntersectionObserver fixed
Pill was hiding too early — it disappeared when the CTA section heading entered the viewport, before the "Inquire About This Piece" button was actually visible. Root cause: observer was targeting #inquire (the whole section) at threshold: 0.2. Fixed by adding id="inquireBtn" to the actual button element and observing that at threshold: 0. Pill now hides only when the button itself enters the viewport. Works correctly across product pages regardless of hero height (DOM-based trigger, not fixed pixel offset). — @bezito
