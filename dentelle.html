The Idea

A Band Drawn as Lace

Most eternity bands defer to the stones. Dentelle inverts the order. The prong itself is the line of the drawing — an arch, then another, then another — and the stones rest in the bays the arches make.

Seen from above, it is a row of light. Seen in profile, it is lace. The same band tells two stories, depending on how she turns her hand.

Dentelle joins the Fenêtres collection in the brand's French archive — two collections that take their names from the architecture they quietly borrow.
