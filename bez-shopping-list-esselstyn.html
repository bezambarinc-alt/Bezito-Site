🛒 Bez's Shopping List

Modified Esselstyn (cardiac plant-forward) · grouped by store aisle · updated 2026-06-05

🥬 Produce — fresh greens (6 servings/day target)

  • Spinach (big bunch — your daily workhorse)
  • Arugula
  • Kale (curly or lacinato)
  • Romaine
  • Swiss chard
  • Parsley (big bunch)

🥕 Produce — vegetables

  • Onions (yellow + red)
  • Garlic (whole heads)
  • Broccoli
  • Cauliflower
  • Cabbage (purple + green)
  • Zucchini
  • Carrots
  • Sweet potato
  • Butternut squash
  • Cucumber
  • Tomato
  • Bell pepper
  • Green beans
  • Mushrooms (cremini, shiitake)
  • Asparagus when in season
  • Brussels sprouts
  • Beets
  • Eggplant

🍎 Produce — fruits

  • Bananas
  • Apples
  • Pears
  • Berries (blueberries, raspberries, strawberries)
  • Citrus (oranges, grapefruit, lemons)
  • Grapes
  • Kiwi
  • Pomegranate (when in season)
  • Cantaloupe / watermelon
  • Mango when in season
  • Avocado (1–2 — not every day, but good fat source)

🥫 Pantry / dry goods

  • Steel-cut oats (breakfast workhorse)
  • Brown rice (long-grain or short-grain)
  • Lentils (green, red, black, beluga — variety pack)
  • Quinoa
  • Freekeh or bulgur
  • Buckwheat
  • Whole-wheat pita
  • Sourdough whole-grain bread
  • Canned chickpeas (multiple)
  • Canned black beans, kidney beans, white beans
  • Hemp hearts (sprinkle on oats — protein boost)
  • Ground flaxseed
  • Hummus (or make your own with chickpeas + tahini)
  • Tahini
  • Salsa / pico de gallo
  • Whole-grain pasta
  • Marinara sauce (no added sugar, low oil)
  • Extra virgin olive oil (good bottle — 1 tbsp/day max for finishing)
  • Balsamic vinegar
  • Apple cider vinegar
  • Dijon mustard
  • Spices: cumin, paprika, turmeric, oregano, basil, black pepper, coarse sea salt
  • Hot sauce if you like

🐟 Fish & protein

  • Wild salmon (1–2 fillets — frozen wild is fine)
  • Sardines (1–2 tins)
  • Sea bass, cod, or halibut if available
  • Mackerel
  • Firm tofu (1 block)
  • Tempeh (1 package)
  • Edamame (frozen, in shell or shelled)
  • Eggs (1 dozen — up to 1/day per updated rule)
  • Skinless chicken breast (1 package — 1× per week max)
  • ❌ Skip: red meat, deli meat, anything processed

🥛 Dairy aisle — mostly skip

  • Unsweetened soy milk (for coffee + oats)
  • Unsweetened oat milk (alternative)
  • Soy yogurt with live cultures (Silk soy yogurt or similar)
  • ❌ Skip: cow's milk, butter, cheese, regular yogurt, cream, sour cream

🌰 Nuts & seeds

  • Walnuts (raw, small handful daily — cardiac-friendly omega-3)
  • Almonds
  • Pumpkin seeds
  • Sunflower seeds
  • Chia seeds
  • Pistachios (small handful)

🍫 Treats (allowed in moderation)

  • Dark chocolate 70%+ — 1 oz/day allowed
  • Dry red wine (1 glass/night max, 3–4 nights/week)
  • Coffee — 1–2 cups before noon
  • Green tea

💊 Supplements

  • Psyllium husk powder (Konsyl plain) — 1 tsp/day in oats or water (lowers LDL + general gut health)
  • Magnesium glycinate 400 mg — your proven sleep formula
  • Vitamin D3 2000 IU
  • Omega-3 / fish oil if not eating fish twice/week
Pro move for travel days: grab 2–3 walnut packets + a couple bananas for the car. Pre-pack snacks = the difference between hitting a Starbucks egg-bite trap and staying on plan.
🚫 Hard skip — don't even walk down these aisles:
  • Red meat (beef, lamb, pork, veal) — zero, this is the single biggest rule
  • Processed meat (bacon, sausage, deli meat, hot dogs, salami, pastrami)
  • Dairy other than soy/oat substitutes
  • Fried foods (chips, fries, schnitzel, donuts)
  • Trans fats / "partially hydrogenated" anything
  • Croissants, danish, donuts, bourekas, pastries
  • Sugar-sweetened drinks, fruit juice in volume
