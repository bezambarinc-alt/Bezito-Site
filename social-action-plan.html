06

Daily Instagram Story cadence

P2
Owner: Darryl + Bez Time: 5-10 min/day Channel: Instagram

Target: minimum 2 Stories/day, ideally 3. Weekly rotation:

  • Mon: Stone-of-the-week close-up
  • Tue: Behind-the-scenes / bench
  • Wed: Repost the day's Reel as Story
  • Thu: Poll or question sticker (e.g., "Pear or oval?")
  • Fri: "Currently making" workshop snippet
  • Sat: Customer wear (UGC if available)
  • Sun: Quote / heritage moment

Critical: archive every Story to the matching Highlight category. Never let good content die at 24 hours.
