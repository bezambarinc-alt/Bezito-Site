Technical Details
Center Stone

Rectangular cushion brilliant-cut diamond, set high in platinum. Crushed-ice faceting pattern — full fire and scintillation across the table, characteristic of the modern cushion brilliant cut.

Hidden Halo

A single row of micro-pavé round brilliant diamonds set into the underside of the bezel, encircling the stone's entire girdle. Invisible from above. A continuous line of light visible at eye level and from the side.

Shank

Two fully independent platinum pavé bands — not a split shank, but two complete arms that begin at the setting base and run parallel the full circumference. A hairline gap of open space between them, consistent from the halo base to the base of the ring. Each arm fully encrusted with small round brilliant micro-pavé diamonds, hairline bead prongs, stone to stone.

Metal

Platinum throughout. High-polish finish on the setting base and cathedral shoulders. Micro-pavé bead-prong setting covering both arms entirely — no polished shank metal visible from any angle.
