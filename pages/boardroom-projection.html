📌 What This Tells You

🇬🇧

Grantham wins if the market keeps going up AND if it crashes at 5-10 years. His non-US heavy portfolio is already beating the US (+17% YTD for VEU). He protects capital in a crash because he has zero US equity. The downside: he never gets the explosive upside if US equity roars back at year 15-20.

🌐

Dalio is never the best, never the worst. The All-Weather portfolio is designed to avoid catastrophe in any environment — not to maximize wealth. If you need to sleep at night, this is the answer. But it always finishes in the middle.

🏦

Buffett is the worst at 5 years in a crash — and the best at 20 years. Here's why: his heavy T-bill position (60% cash) takes a smaller hit in the crash, then he deploys all that cash to buy beaten-down American companies at the bottom. He's literally waiting for this. By year 20 in a crash scenario, he ends up with $8.2M — more than anyone else, in either scenario.

⚠️

The question is your time horizon and risk tolerance. If you need this money in 5 years → Grantham protects best in both scenarios. If this is truly 15-20 year money (legacy / estate) → Buffett wins, especially if a crash happens and you can hold through it.
