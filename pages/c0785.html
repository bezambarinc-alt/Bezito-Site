Zenith

This exceptional pendant centers a fancy intense yellow cushion-cut diamond, held in yellow gold prongs and encircled by a close-set halo of colorless round brilliant diamonds. Tapered baguettes radiate outward in a full sunburst, contained within a cushion-square border of round brilliants set in white gold. Suspended from a yellow gold spiga chain — the architecture is total.
